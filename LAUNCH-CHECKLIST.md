# Business information required before launch

- [ ] Approved business phone, country-code Booking number, email, complete address, opening hours, map embed and actual social URLs.
- [ ] Company introduction, travel approach, team information and service locations based only on supplied facts.
- [ ] Business-approved reasons to choose the company; no unverified years, statistics, awards or certifications.
- [ ] Every package's approved name, destination(s), actual duration, trip type, itinerary and options.
- [ ] Package-specific accommodation, transport, meals, inclusions/exclusions and cancellation terms. Leave unconfirmed benefits absent.
- [ ] Confirmed starting prices/currency or Request Quote; agreed details of how final quotations are confirmed.
- [ ] Licensed travel photography with correct alternative text and source/ownership records.
- [ ] Actual vehicle photographs, approved model/category, seating, AC/non-AC, supplied luggage specifications, confirmed amenities and rental terms.
- [ ] Approved service descriptions for family/honeymoon, college visits, school trips, corporate/devotional/adventure tours.
- [ ] Authentic reviews verified against business records, with internal verification notes. No fabricated testimonials.
- [ ] Reviewed Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Cancellation and Refund Policy, including enquiry retention/deletion contact and WhatsApp third-party disclosure.
- [ ] Remove draft labels and confirm the actual sample duration before publishing any seeded package. Seed duration of 1 is an unpublished technical placeholder, not a promise.
- [ ] Configure production HTTPS, unique admin credentials and dedicated database user. Create the administrator through one-time setup; remove/rotate the installation token afterward.
- [ ] Verify Booking destinations/messages with the actual approved number; visitors still send messages themselves.
- [ ] Test website enquiries and admin updates on hosting; enquiries must not be presented as confirmed bookings.
- [ ] Check desktop/tablet/mobile, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, WebGL fallback, gallery previews, private directory denial and upload execution protection on the deployed server.
- [ ] Confirm backups, data retention, hosting error-log access and who is responsible for business approvals.
